How to save for retirement as a graduate student (in the US)?
Graduate stipend is limited, but starting financial planning and compound investing in your 20s will produce an astonishing compound effect over the coming decades.
Sharing some financial tips for graduate students. 🧵

研究生在读期间如何规划财务与退休储蓄（以美国为例）？
研究生的津贴（Stipend）虽然有限，但在二十多岁尽早开始财务规划与复利投资，会在未来几十年产生惊人的复利效应。  
分享面向研究生的财务经验。🧵

---

*Power of compound interest*
Even saving $100-$200 a month will yield far greater returns over a 20-30 year horizon compared to someone who only starts in their 30s.

*复利的力量与时间价值 (Power of compound interest)*
哪怕每个月只存 100-200 美元，由于二三十年的漫长投资期，其最终收益也远超三十多岁才开始起步的人。

---

*Roth IRA*
Because grad students are typically in a lower income tax bracket, opening a Roth IRA (after-tax contribution, tax-free withdrawal in retirement) provides huge tax advantages.

*善用 Roth IRA 个人退休账户 (Roth IRA)*
由于研究生期间收入相对较低、处于较低的个人所得税税率区间（Tax Bracket），开设 Roth IRA（税后注资、未来提领免税）具有极高的税务优势。

---

*Emergency fund first*
Before investing in stocks or index funds, maintain 3-6 months of basic living expenses in a High-Yield Savings Account (HYSA) for unexpected health or moving expenses.

*建立紧急备用金 (Emergency fund first)*
在进行任何股票或指数基金投资前，首先在高息储蓄账户（HYSA）中存留 3-6 个月的基本生活开支，以应对突发的医疗或搬迁开销。

---

*Low-cost index funds*
Avoid day trading or chasing individual stock hypes. Opt for low-fee S&P 500 or total market index funds (e.g., VOO / VTI), focus on your research, and let assets grow passively.

*坚持低成本宽基指数基金定投 (Low-cost index funds)*
避免追涨杀跌或炒个股。选择低费率的标普 500 或全球股票指数基金（如 VOO / VTI），专注于科研主业，让资产被动增长。
