How to present a poster at a conference?

Poster presentation is a great way to discuss your work one-on-one with fellow researchers.

Here are some tips to make your poster sessions engaging and productive! 🧵

如何在学术会议上进行海报讲解？

海报展示是与同行学者进行一对一深度交流探讨的极佳方式。

这里有一些让你的海报展示环节充满吸引力且卓有成效的建议！🧵

---

*Prepare multiple pitches*

Not everyone has the same amount of time or background.  
Prepare:  
• 30-second elevator pitch (high-level takeaway)  
• 2-minute summary (problem + method + main result)  
• 5-10 minute deep dive (for domain experts)

*准备多种时长的宣讲版本 (Prepare multiple pitches)*

并不是每个走过来的参会者都拥有相同的时间或专业背景。  
准备好：  
• 30 秒电梯演讲（宏观核心突破）  
• 2 分钟精简版（问题 + 核心方法 + 主要结果）  
• 5-10 分钟深度剖析（面向同领域的资深同行）

---

*Engage the audience*

Don't just launch into a monologue!  
Ask them: "Are you familiar with this field?" or "What kind of problems do you work on?"  
Tailor your explanation to their interests.

*与听众积极互动 (Engage the audience)*

不要自顾自地进行单向背书！  
主动询问：“您之前了解过这个方向吗？”或者“您平时主要从事哪个领域的研究？”  
根据对方的背景和兴趣定制你的讲解重点。

---

*Point to the poster*

Use your hands and body language to guide their eyes through the poster.  
"As you can see in Figure 2..."  
Keep them visually engaged with the content.

*手势引导视线 (Point to the poster)*

用肢体语言和手势引导他们的视线穿梭于海报之间。  
“正如大家在图 2 中所看到的……”  
让他们的视线始终与你的讲解焦点保持同步。
