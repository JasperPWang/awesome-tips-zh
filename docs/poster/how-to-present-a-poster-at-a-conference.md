# How to present a poster at a conference? / 如何在学术会议中专业地讲解海报？

How to present a poster at a conference?
Poster presentation is a great way to disseminate your research findings, have in-depth discussions, and expand your networks!
Here are some tips for presenting like a pro! 👇

如何在学术会议中专业地讲解海报？
海报展示是传播你的科研成果、与同行展开深度学术探讨并拓展学术人脉的绝佳契机！
分享一些让你像专业学者一样讲解海报的秘诀！👇

---

*Be Welcoming*
✅ Stand by the side of the poster.
✅ Never face the poster and turn your back on your audience.
✅ Actively approach people walking by.

*展现热情迎客的姿态 (Be Welcoming)*
✅ 站在海报的侧面。
✅ 绝不要面对海报而背对观众。
✅ 主动与路过的学者眼神接触并打招呼。

![Be welcoming](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FRFd9Z0WUAA7C1F.jpg)

---

*Be Enthusiastic*
If you do not sound excited about your work, why should others be excited?
Hype yourself up! 🤗
People can feel the positive energy and will be willing to learn more about your work.

*充满激情 (Be Enthusiastic)*
如果你自己聊起自己的研究都提不起劲，别人凭什么为你感到兴奋？
让自己燃起来！🤗
大家能清晰感受到积极的正能量，并因此更渴望了解你的工作。

![Be enthusiastic](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FRFd91kWYAEiC4z.jpg)

---

*Be Early and Stay Late*
There are fewer people *before* and *after* the scheduled poster session.
Don't miss the great opportunity to have interesting, long conversation with people genuinely interested in your work.

*早到迟退 (Be Early and Stay Late)*
在正式的海报交流时段“之前”和“之后”，走廊里的人流相对较少。
千万不要错过这些与真正对你工作感兴趣的学者展开长谈的绝佳窗口。

![Early and late](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FRFd-RLWUAAVAoR.jpg)

---

*Be Concise*
❌ Present linearly from top to bottom for 10 mins.
✅ Present an elevator pitch for 30 seconds. Hook the audience and then respond to any questions.

*精简精炼 (Be Concise)*
❌ 从上到下线性平铺直叙讲上足足 10 分钟。
✅ 用 30 秒进行精炼的电梯演讲。牢牢吸引住观众，然后根据对方的具体关切解答提问。

![Be concise](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FRFd-toXIAwDcLk.jpg)

---

*Use Tools*
Poster is just a *static* visual abstract.
If your work involve other *dynamic* mediums (e.g., video, sound), bringing a laptop or tablet would make your presentation a lot more engaging.

*善用辅助工具 (Use Tools)*
海报只是一份“静态”的视觉摘要。
如果你的研究涉及其他“动态”媒介（如视频生成、音频合成），带上一台笔记本电脑或平板电脑，会使你的海报讲解更加引人入胜。

![Use tools](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FRFd_JkXsAAndyC.jpg)

---

*No "Do you know X?"*
Yes, your intention is good, but this only makes other people feel dumb.
Give a 10 sec background about X and then focus on *your work*.

*别问“你知道 X 吗？” (No "Do you know X?")*
虽然你的出发点是好的，但这往往会让提问者感到窘迫。
花 10 秒钟快速介绍 X 的背景，然后把焦点集中在**你自己的工作**上。

![No do you know](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FRFd_k5XsAE5EvB.jpg)

---

*Handout*
Print several mini-poster as handout so that you can give people who are interested in learning more.
Make sure that the poster has link/barcode that refer them to your project page.

*打印随手宣传彩页 (Handout)*
打印若干张 A4 大小的迷你缩印海报作为宣传单，发给那些表现出浓厚兴趣的学者。
确保彩页上附有清晰的链接或二维码，直接引导他们访问你的项目主页。

![Handout](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FRFeAA9XEAAwO0F.jpg)

---

*Build Your Network*
• Thank *each* person for stopping by your poster and/or asking good questions.
• Say their name (or ask how to pronounce their name).
👉 Now you can greet them next time and grow your connection.

*拓展学术人脉 (Build Your Network)*
• 向*每一位*驻足海报或提出优质问题的学者真诚致谢。
• 叫出他们的名字（或礼貌询问他们名字的发音）。
👉 这样下次在会场遇到他们时你就能自然打招呼，持续拓展人脉。

![Build network](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FRFeAcLWYAEZmlx.jpg)

---

*Bring People to Your Poster*
Senior folks: Bring other senior people you know to your students' poster!
Nothing is more validating than presenting your work to researchers whom you look up to.

*带同行来自己学生的海报前 (Bring People to Your Poster)*
资深学者们：把你认识的其他学术大牛带到你学生的海报前！
没有什么比能亲自向自己仰慕的顶尖学者展示研究成果更让年轻学生备受鼓舞的了。

![Bring people](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FRFeA4PWUAA_HkT.jpg)

*制作一张出色的海报是基础 (Make a Good Poster)*
想要做好海报讲解，你首先需要拥有一张设计精良的海报！
如果海报本身制作糟糕，再好的口头表达技巧也无济于事。

*Share Feedbacks with Co-Authors*
Document the feedbacks and questions right after the poster presentation session.
Share with your co-authors so that they all can learn from it.

*与共同作者分享反馈 (Share Feedbacks with Co-Authors)*
在海报交流环节结束后，第一时间记录下同行提出的所有反馈与疑问。
与共同作者们分享讨论，让整个研究团队都能从中汲取经验。

![Share feedback](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FRFeBvmX0AAuCjY.jpg)
