How to Set Up a Good Calendar Invite?

Our days are filled with events on the calendar (e.g., project meetings, roundtable, committee, 1:1s) 🗓️ 

But, most calendar invites are awfully vague and lead to unproductive meetings. 

Five tips on writing a good calendar invite 👇

如何发起一个规范的日历邀请？

我们每天的日程都被日历填满（项目会议、圆桌讨论、委员会、1:1 对话等）🗓️。

然而，绝大多数日历邀请都模糊得令人痛苦，从而导致会议效率低下。

分享写好日历邀请的 5 个关键技巧。👇

---

*Descriptive Title*  
❌ "Meeting" or "Chat"  
✅ "[Project Name] Discussion - Alice / Bob"

*具备描述性的标题 (Descriptive Title)*  
❌ “Meeting” 或 “Chat”  
✅ “[项目名] 讨论 - 张三 / 李四”

---

*Working Meeting Link*  
Always include a clickable Zoom/Google Meet link in the location field so nobody wastes time searching for where to join.

*有效的会议链接 (Working Meeting Link)*  
始终在地点（Location）栏中包含一个可直接点击的 Zoom / Google Meet 链接，这样没人会浪费时间到处寻找参会入口。

---

*Clear Agenda*  
List 2-3 specific bullet points describing what will be discussed and decided during the meeting.

*清晰的会议议程 (Clear Agenda)*  
列出 2-3 个具体的项目符号，明确说明本次会议将要讨论和决策的核心议题。

---

*Attach Materials*  
Add links to slides, Overleaf documents, or notes directly in the invite description.

*附带关键材料 (Attach Materials)*  
将需要讨论的 Slide、Overleaf 论文草稿或笔记链接直接粘贴在邀请描述中。

---

*Respect Time Zones*  
Double-check the recipient's local time zone before sending. Avoid early mornings or late evenings unless agreed upon.

*尊重彼此时区 (Respect Time Zones)*  
发送前务必二次确认对方所在的本地时区，避免约在对方清晨或深夜。
