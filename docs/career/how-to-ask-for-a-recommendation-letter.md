# How to ask for a letter of recommendation? / 如何请求导师写推荐信？

> 原文作者：Jia-Bin Huang ([@jbhuang0604](https://x.com/jbhuang0604))

---

How to Ask for a Letter of Recommendation?
Another round of graduate school, award/fellowship, job applications is coming!
Recommendation letter is arguably THE most critical component in your application package.
How do you get a strong letter? 🤔

如何请求写推荐信？
新一轮的研究生申请、奖学金/奖项评选、求职申请即将到来！
推荐信可以说是整个申请材料包中最为核心的关键组件。
你该如何获得一封强有力的推荐信？🤔

---

*It's their Job*
Many students are afraid to ask for letters and think the faculty is *doing them a favor*.
It's *their job*! (They probably have asked for hundreds of letters to get their current role.)
👉 If a faculty can provide a detailed assessment about you, just ask!

*这是教授的本职工作 (It's their Job)*
许多学生不敢开口要推荐信，觉得这是在求教授“帮个人情”。
这本就是教授的“职责所在”！（教授自己当年为了拿到教职，可能也请求过成百上千封推荐信。）
👉 只要这位教授能够对你做出详实具体的评价，大胆开口请求即可！

---

*Ask Early*
Writing a letter takes time, a lot of time.
👉 Make sure that you give the faculty ample amount of time to procrastinate!

*尽早提出请求 (Ask Early)*
写一封高质量的推荐信需要耗费大量时间。
👉 请确保给教授留出充足的拖延时间（procrastinate）！

---

*Ask Often*
Writing a letter from scratch is hard.
Revising a letter is a lot easier.
👉 Ask for letters for earlier opportunities so that the letter for you will be increasingly polished over time.

*多创造写信机会 (Ask Often)*
从零开始起草一封推荐信非常艰难。
而在已有底稿的基础上修改则轻松得多。
👉 在早期的各类机会中就积极请求推荐信，这样随着时间的推移，教授写你的推荐信会越来越凝练出彩。

---

*Ask Specifically*
Tell the letter writer what you wish they can cover (e.g., your involvement on X project? your contribution on Y)?
Provide all the material (e.g., SOP, CV, your project, due dates).
👉 Make their job easier!

*明确具体期望 (Ask Specifically)*
清晰告知写信人你希望他们在信中重点涵盖哪些内容（例如：你在 X 项目中的参与度？你在 Y 课题上的核心贡献？）。
主动提供所有配套材料（个人陈述 SOP、简历 CV、项目材料、截止日期清单）。
👉 让写信人的工作变得更轻松！

---

*Ask for Calibration*
Many letter writers only write generic descriptions. However, who isn't hard-working, highly motivated, and a team-player?
👉 Make sure that your letter provides some sorts of calibrations (among peers in your institutes or previous applicants).

*请求提供校准基准 (Ask for Calibration)*
很多推荐人只会写千篇一律的套话。然而，哪个申请人不努力、不上进、不具备团队合作精神呢？
👉 确保你的推荐信包含某种形式的横向校准基准（例如：与本校同届学生相比排前 X%、或与往届录取者对比）。

---

*Follow Up*
Send out friendly reminder so that your letter writers won't forget to upload your letter (and answer many stupid questions).
Keep track of whether the letters have been submitted.
👉 Help your letter writers stay sane!

*礼貌跟进提醒 (Follow Up)*
发送友善的提醒邮件，避免推荐人忙昏头忘记上传推荐信（顺便还要填写各类繁琐问卷）。
时刻追踪系统里的推荐信提交通知。
👉 帮你的推荐人保持清醒从容！

---

*Share the News*
Seeing their mentee winning an award, landing a job, or getting an offer for grad school is extremely rewarding!
👉 Remember to share the results and thank your letter writers!

*同步申请结果 (Share the News)*
亲眼见证自己指导的学生斩获大奖、拿到心仪教职或收到顶尖学府 Offer，是为人师表最有成就感的时刻！
👉 拿到结果后一定要告诉推荐人并表达真挚谢意！

---

I hope this is helpful.
Best of luck for getting your dream job, school, and award!!

希望这些建议能对大家有所启发。
预祝大家都能顺利斩获理想的工作、学校录取和荣誉大奖！！
