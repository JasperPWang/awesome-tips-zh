How to find research opportunities?
Finding opportunities to gain experience is arguably the most challenging part for students wishing to pursue grad school, particularly for those who don't have resources/connections.
Some tips on approaching potential mentors. 🧵

如何寻找科研机会？
对于希望申请研究生的同学来说，寻找积累科研经验的机会可以说是最具挑战性的环节，尤其是对于那些缺乏现成资源和人脉的同学。
分享一些主动联系潜在学术导师的技巧。🧵

---

*Lead with value*
❌ "I am very interested ... I want to grow ... I want to get the experiences... I have many prior experiences ... me, me, me, me, me, me, me!"
✅ "Here is what I can contribute to your team. I can do this bc my experience/skills on blah blah blah."

*以价值为导向 (Lead with value)*
❌ “我非常感兴趣……我想成长……我想积累经验……我有很多过往经历……我、我、我、我、我、我、我！”
✅ “这是我能为您的团队带来的贡献。我之所以能做这件事，是因为我在某某领域拥有扎实的经验/技能。”

---

*Go the extra mile*
❌ Generic email sent to 500 professors. "I found your project X inspiring."
✅ "I ran the code from your latest project X. Here are some limitations I found. I think we can explore this and that direction to tackle the problem."

*多走一步，展现超常投入 (Go the extra mile)*
❌ 给 500 位教授群发通用套磁信：“我觉得您的 X 项目深受启发。”
✅ “我跑了您最新项目 X 的开源代码，发现了一些局限性。我认为我们可以尝试从这两个方向来攻克这个难题。”

---

*Make it easy to say yes*
❌ "I want to work with you this semester. Can we schedule a one-hour zoom meeting to tell me what project I can work on?"
✅ "I want to work on problem X. Here is the doc of initial explorations and my plan. Would you give me some feedback?"

*让对方更容易给出肯定答复 (Make it easy to say yes)*
❌ “我想这学期跟您做科研。我们能否安排一小时的 Zoom 会议，您告诉我有什么项目可以让我做？”
✅ “我想攻克 X 问题。附上我前期的初步探索文档与执行计划。请问您能给我一些指导反馈吗？”

---

*Show your work (in public)*
❌ A resume of a long list of various projects you work on.
✅ A website documenting your work at different stages (as code, report, slides, poster, video, paper).

*公开展示你的研究成果 (Show your work in public)*
❌ 简历上罗列一长串花里胡哨的项目名称列表。
✅ 建立一个个人网站，完整记录各个阶段的真实成果（包含开源代码、技术报告、幻灯片、海报、演示视频、论文）。

---

*Do the best work you can (when you have an opportunity)*
❌ Do an 8-week internship dip. Collect the "experience". Move on to yet another research opportunity.
✅ Work hard. Complete and document your project. Doing good work naturally leads to many more opportunities.

*一旦获得机会，全力做到最好 (Do the best work you can)*
❌ 混一个 8 周的实习，仅仅为了简历上增加一段“经历”，随后又跳去下一个地方。
✅ 踏实努力，完整交付并整理归档你的项目。扎实优秀的工作成果自然会为你带来更多源源不断的机会。

---

Hope this helps! And hope everyone gets their dream opportunity to learn and grow.
Best of luck!

希望这些建议能有所帮助！祝愿每个人都能获得梦寐以求的科研机会，不断学习成长。
祝大家好运！
