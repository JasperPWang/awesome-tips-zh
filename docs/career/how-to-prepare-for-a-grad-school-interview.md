How to prepare graduate school interview?
After refreshing GradCafe pages and your email sufficient number of times, you are getting an 🎊INTERVIEW🎊 with your potential advisor at your dream university! 🤯
Congrats! But how to do well in those interviews? Some tips below 🧵

如何准备研究生面试？
在把 GradCafe 页面和邮箱刷新了无数次之后，你终于收到了心仪大学意向导师发来的🎊面试邀请🎊！🤯
祝贺你！但如何在这些面试中发挥出色？分享以下建议 🧵

---

*Be prepared*
Some faculty may say "it's just chatting". THEY LIED! They will seriously evaluate the interaction with you during the call and form their opinions.
Prepare slides summarizing your work and practice commonly asked questions (e.g., research, goals, why here).

*做好充分准备 (Be prepared)*
有些教授可能会说“这只是随便聊聊”。他们在说谎！他们会在通话过程中认真评估与你的每一次互动，并形成自己的判断。
准备好总结你过往工作的幻灯片，并演练常见问题（例如：研究经历、未来目标、为什么选择这里）。

![Be prepared](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FJH4bdLWUAAWchI.jpg)

---

*Express interest*
Dept. often can only admit a limited number of applicants. So they have to strategize who to give out the offers. The level of interests you express plays an important role. You won't get an offer if the dept. feels that the chance of recruiting you is low.

*表达浓厚兴趣 (Express interest)*
系里通常只能录取有限数量的申请者，因此他们必须运筹帷幄把录取名额（Offers）发给谁。你所表达的兴趣程度起着关键作用。如果系里觉得你接受录取的概率很低，他们就不会给你发 Offer。

![Express interest](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FJH4b6lXMAA60Zk.jpg)

---

*Be organized*
The email correspondences with the faculty send out signals of how organized you are. Send concise, clear, well-formatted emails. Share your availability with specific time slots (converted to their timezone). Help them better manage their time.

*展现严谨条理 (Be organized)*
与教授的邮件往来，直接传递出你做事是否有条理的信号。发送简明、清晰、排版得体的邮件。提供几个具体的可用时间段（并主动换算为对方所在的时区），帮助教授更好地管理时间。

![Be organized](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FJH4cYmXoAQE9iO.jpg)

---

*Ask questions*
The interview is bi-directional. You are also interviewing with the faculty/university. Do your homework. Ask specific questions that matter most to you.
Sample questions:
https://drive.google.com/file/d/1o2ZTrU6M_m472dM5L2D_hJm7P0cI1p7p/view

*主动提问 (Ask questions)*
面试是双向的。你同时也在考察这位教授和这所大学。提前做好功课，提出对你来说最为关键的具体问题。
参考问题清单：
https://drive.google.com/file/d/1o2ZTrU6M_m472dM5L2D_hJm7P0cI1p7p/view

![Ask questions](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FJH4dFCXsAIN2ij.jpg)

---

*Be yourself*
Don't need to freak out. You don't need to change anything or wear formal suit for these interviews. The potential advisor is interested in knowing about you and your research. Show your true color.

*做真实的自己 (Be yourself)*
完全不需要惊慌失措。你不需要刻意改变什么，这类面试也无需西装革履。意向导师关心的是了解真正的你以及你的科研潜力。展现出你的本色即可。

![Be yourself](https://pbs.twimg.com/ext_tw_video_thumb/1482238515194777609/pu/img/k9b7gdmQFTBWa-bB.jpg)
