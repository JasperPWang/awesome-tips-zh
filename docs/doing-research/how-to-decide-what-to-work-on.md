How to decide what to work on?

So many exciting ideas, so little time! How can one narrow down to a specific idea to work on? 🤔

Sharing some strategies I found useful. 🧵

如何决定研究什么课题？

令人兴奋的想法如此之多，而时间却如此有限！一个人该如何聚焦并筛选出一个具体值得投入的课题？🤔

分享一些我认为很有用的策略。🧵

---

*Find your position*

Understand your unfair advantages over others, what the community cares about, and how you can best contribute.  
Avoid crowding the ball (aka chasing the hottest problem in the field).

*找准你的定位 (Find your position)*

清晰认识你相对于其他人的“不对称优势（unfair advantages）”、学术共同体真正关心的是什么、以及你怎样才能做出最好的贡献。  
避免扎堆抢球（Avoid crowding the ball，即不要一味追逐当下领域里最热门的问题）。

---

*Identify intrinsic motivation*

Work on problems and real applications that you genuinely care about.

*挖掘内在动力 (Identify intrinsic motivation)*

去研究那些你自己真心关切、真正渴望解决的问题与现实应用。

---

*Anticipate disruptive technologies*

Instead of focusing on what everyone is working on NOW, anticipate the major problems LATER when disruptive technologies (e.g., new sensors, algorithms, models, data) emerge.

*预见颠覆性技术 (Anticipate disruptive technologies)*

不要只专注于眼下所有人正在做的事情，而是去预判当颠覆性技术（例如：新传感器、新算法、新模型、新数据集）涌现**之后**，未来将面临哪些重大挑战。

---

*Imagine success*

At the begging of your project, be an amateur and dream big! Don't be intimidated by all the technical challenges just yet.  
"Think like an amateur, do as an expert" - Takeo Kanade.

*设想成功 (Imagine success)*

在项目启动之初，像外行一样敢于做大梦！暂时不要被眼下所有的技术挑战吓退。  
正如金出武雄（Takeo Kanade）教授所言：“像外行一样思考，像专家一样实践（Think like an amateur, do as an expert）”。

---

*Validate the idea fast*

Not all the ideas will pan out. Design small experiments to validate your hypothesis and assumptions.  
If you fail, fail FAST!

*快速验证想法 (Validate the idea fast)*

并不是所有的想法都能最终开花结果。设计极小的实验来快速验证你的假设与前置条件。  
如果要失败，请**快速失败（fail FAST）**！

---

These strategies have been helpful for me in prioritizing multiple ideas.  
Hope these give you clarity on what you should work on as well.

在面对多个想法并进行优先级排序时，这些策略对我非常有帮助。  
希望这些也能让你在决定研究什么课题时拥有更清晰的思路。
