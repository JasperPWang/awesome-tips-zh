# How to work with a busy advisor? / 如何与非常繁忙的导师高效合作？

> 原文作者：Jia-Bin Huang ([@jbhuang0604](https://x.com/jbhuang0604))  
> 原始推文：[https://twitter.com/jbhuang0604/status/1665183152887808000](https://twitter.com/jbhuang0604/status/1665183152887808000)

---

How to work with a busy advisor?
Your advisor constantly needs to juggle many tasks 🤹 (family/teaching/research/service).  
In some cases, students only get to meet with their advisor once/twice a semester and thus struggle to make progress. 😬
Some tips I found helpful. 🧵

如何与非常繁忙的导师高效合作？
你的导师经常需要同时兼顾许多繁重事务 🤹（家庭/教学/科研/学术服务）。  
在某些情况下，学生一个学期只能见到导师一两次，从而导致科研进展停滞不前。😬
分享一些我认为非常有帮助的建议。🧵

---

*Async check-ins*
Establish a regular weekly asynchronous update channel (e.g., a short Notion page or email) if in-person meetings are hard to schedule.
Help your advisor understand your progress in 3 minutes while they are at the airport or commuting.

*建立固定的异步沟通管道 (Async check-ins)*
如果难以频繁约到线下面对面会议，建立一个固定的每周异步更新机制（如简短的 Notion 页面或邮件）。
让导师哪怕在机场候机或通勤路上，也能用 3 分钟快速了解你的核心进展。

---

*Micro-meetings*
Take advantage of the 5-10 minutes before and after group meetings, or casual hallway encounters, to quickly confirm 1-2 key decision points.

*抓住微小时间窗口 (Micro-meetings)*
善用组会前后的 5-10 分钟，或者在导师办公室门口的偶遇，快速确认 1-2 个最关键的决策点。

---

*Take the initiative to schedule*
Don't just wait for your advisor to reach out. Proactively send a calendar invite with a clear agenda when reaching important milestones.

*主动推动排期 (Take the initiative to schedule)*
不要被动等待导师找你。在关键节点主动发起日历邀请，并提供明确的会议议程。

---

*Cultivate independence*
Busy advisors appreciate students who can independently drive projects forward. Reliable execution earns you more trust and research freedom.

*保持自主性与独立科研能力 (Cultivate independence)*
繁忙的导师最欣赏能够独立推进项目、自我驱动的学生。当你展示出可靠的执行力时，导师会给予你更多信任与科研自由度。
