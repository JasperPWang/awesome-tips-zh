# How to start a presentation? / 如何开始一场学术演讲？

> 原文作者：Jia-Bin Huang ([@jbhuang0604](https://x.com/jbhuang0604))  
> 原始推文：[https://twitter.com/jbhuang0604/status/1548882661581754370](https://twitter.com/jbhuang0604/status/1548882661581754370)

How to start a presentation?
Most people start with all the boring info (e.g., repeating everything your host just said) and unmotivated technical details. 🥱
After a few mins, everyone starts checking their phone! 😱
How to avoid this? Here is a simple template. 🧵

如何开始一场学术演讲？
大多数人开场都会讲一堆枯燥乏味的信息（比如把主持人刚才介绍你的话原样复述一遍）以及缺乏背景铺垫的技术细节。🥱
仅仅过了几分钟，台下的听众就开始纷纷低头刷手机了！😱
如何避免这种情况？这里有一个简单实用的开场模板。🧵

---

*Hook*
❌"Hi, my name is .. I am a .. from .. My research .. I am happy to be here. Today I am going to present this title on the slide."
✅ Start your talk with a story, a picture, a surprising statistics, a quote, a question, a poll from audience, or a guessing game.

*抓住眼球的“钩子” (Hook)*
❌“大家好，我叫……，我是来自……的……，我的研究方向是……，我很高兴来到这里。今天我要讲的是幻灯片上的这个标题。”
✅ 用一个引人入胜的故事、一张震撼的图片、一组出人意料的统计数据、一句名人名言、一个发人深省的问题、一次现场听众投票或一个小猜谜游戏来拉开序幕。

![Hook](https://pbs.twimg.com/ext_tw_video_thumb/1548866994237313024/pu/img/qOgvMRX8MaMOaYQf.jpg)

---

*Transition*
Once you got everyone's attention with your hook, smoothly transit to the topic you want to talk about.
"Now why do I tell you this story?"
"Why do I show this particular picture?"
"I see most of you raised your hand. But in fact ..."

*平滑过渡 (Transition)*
一旦你用“钩子”抓住了所有人的注意力，便顺理成章地平滑过渡到你今天要讲的主题上：
“那我为什么要给大家讲这个故事呢？”
“我为什么要特意展示这张图片？”
“我看到刚才大部分人都举手了，但实际上……”

![Transition](https://pbs.twimg.com/ext_tw_video_thumb/1548876716705185793/pu/img/cN2-V0FWZMU3XeN0.jpg)

---

*Preview*
Now you have talked about WHY you want to talk about your topic with your hook and transition, give a preview of WHAT you are going to talk about.
More on how to organize your talk:
https://twitter.com/jbhuang0604/status/1509033327981965313

*内容预览 (Preview)*
通过“钩子”和“过渡”，你已经阐明了**为什么（WHY）**要讨论这个主题，接下来给出你要讲的具体**内容概要（WHAT）**。
关于如何组织演讲架构的更多建议：
https://twitter.com/jbhuang0604/status/1509033327981965313

![Preview](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FX68niaWQAAsmQt.jpg)

---

*Benefit for your audience*
Convince why your audience should spend the next 40 mins of their life (that they cannot get back) listening to your talk.
What questions will you answer? What will they learn? How will your talk benefit the audience?

*明确听众能获得什么收益 (Benefit for your audience)*
说服你的听众：他们为什么要拿出人生中无法倒流的宝贵 40 分钟来听你的报告？
你将回答什么核心问题？他们能学到什么新认知？你的演讲能为他们带来什么实际收获？

![Benefit for your audience](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FX68oAiX0AEGJGd.jpg)
