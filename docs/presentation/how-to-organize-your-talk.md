# How to organize your talk? / 如何组织你的学术汇报架构？

How to organize your talk?
Qual/prelim/defense/conference season is coming up! 😱 How should we organize the talk so that your committee members won't fall asleep?
Some simple tips I found useful ...👇

如何组织学术汇报？
博士资格考 / 开题答辩 / 毕业答辩 / 会议季即将来临！😱 我们该如何组织汇报架构，才能让考核委员会的评审教授们全程保持清醒不打瞌睡？
分享一些我发现非常管用的小技巧……👇

---

*Outline*
Organizing your talk with an OUTLINE slide?
I couldn't think of a weaker way of starting presentation with an outline! 🥱
Instead, start the presentation by explaining
👉 WHY SHOULD THEY CARE?
Describe why the problem is important, interesting, and challenging.

*别用平庸的大纲页开场 (Outline)*
还在用传统的“大纲（Outline）”幻灯片来开篇？
我想不出还有比一上来就念大纲更催眠、更无力的开场方式了！🥱
相反，汇报开场必须开门见山阐述：
👉 **他们为什么要关心这个问题？（WHY SHOULD THEY CARE?）**
深刻阐述为什么这个科学问题如此重要、有趣且充满挑战。

---

*Menu. Not outline*
After motivating your talk, show a MENU slide that consists of two or three Entrée (topics that you want to cover in your talk).
Why?
👉 because the menu provides an easy way to navigate your talk.

*使用“菜单”，而非死板大纲 (Menu. Not outline)*
在点燃大家对课题的兴趣之后，展示一张“菜单（MENU）”页，列出两到三个“主菜（Entrée）”（即你本次汇报涵盖的核心主题）。
为什么？
👉 因为菜单能为整场汇报提供极其清晰、生动的导航路标。

---

*Tell them what you are going to say*
Present all the Entrée options, then highlight the first one (and don't forget to de-emphasize the remaining ones).
This helps people know where are we heading to.

*告诉他们你正要讲什么 (Tell them what you are going to say)*
呈现所有主菜选项后，高亮突出第一道主菜（别忘了将其他选项弱化置灰）。
这能让听众时刻清楚知道我们正驶向何方。

---

*Say it*
Now you have set the stage. Just say it!
Here you can use as many slides as you want to introduce the first Entrée.

*开讲第一部分 (Say it)*
舞台已经铺就，直接开讲！
在这里你可以用足够充实的幻灯片来深入介绍第一道主菜的技术细节。

---

*Tell them what you've said*
Next, after finishing the first part, zoom back out to the *same* menu slide.
✅ Summarize what you've just said
✅ Explain why we need to check out second Entrée (remember to emphasize/de-emphasize different Entrées)
Then repeat the 3-steps.

*回顾刚才讲过的内容 (Tell them what you've said)*
讲完第一部分后，重新拉回视线，回到那张**同一张菜单幻灯片**：
✅ 总结刚才第一部分的结论
✅ 阐明为什么接下来需要品尝第二道主菜（记得高亮第二项并置灰其他项）
随后重复这三步节奏。

---

*Final slide*
Do NOT show a meaningless "thank you slide".
At the end of your talk, bring back your MENU slide so that people can order (aka asking good questions) easily.

*最后一页幻灯片 (Final slide)*
千万不要放空洞无物的“Thank you”致谢页。
在汇报结尾，重新把你的“菜单（MENU）”页亮出来，方便评委和听众对照点餐（即提出高质量的好问题）。
