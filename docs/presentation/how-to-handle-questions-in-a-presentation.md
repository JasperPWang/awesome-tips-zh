# How to handle questions in a presentation? / 如何在学术演讲中从容应对提问？

How to handle questions in a presentation?
When I was a student, I always feel stressful during the Q&A session after my presentation. 😵‍💫 In many cases, I didn't know how to respond well and just froze up there! 🥶
Sharing some tips I learned from others. 🧵

如何在学术演讲中从容应对提问？
当我还是个学生的时候，每次演讲后的 Q&A 问答环节总让我压力倍增。😵‍💫 很多时候我根本不知道该怎么回答，整个人直接僵在台上！🥶
分享一些我从前辈身上学到的技巧。🧵

---

*Reframe the question*
Repeating and summarizing the key questions help
1⃣ ensure the audience feel heard,
2⃣ confirm that you are answering the right Q,
3⃣ let other audience understand what the Q is, and
4⃣ give yourself a bit buffer to think and plan your answer.

*重述并确认问题 (Reframe the question)*
复述并归纳核心问题有助于：
1⃣ 让提问者感受到自己的问题被认真倾听并尊重；
2⃣ 确认你回答的是对方真正问的核心点；
3⃣ 让全场其他听众也能听清楚这个问题是什么；
4⃣ 为你自己争取宝贵的缓冲思考时间以组织答案。

![Reframe question](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FY9wZ9VXkAY_yUN.jpg)

---

*Respond the question concisely*
Address the question directly and concisely. Don't spend 1 min flipping through your slides trying to find the exact slide. 😵‍💫
When you
- know your shit 👉 Answer concisely.
- don't know the ans. 👉 Admit you don't know and commit to follow up.

*简明扼要作答 (Respond concisely)*
直接了当地回答问题，切忌花整整一分钟疯狂狂翻幻灯片试图找到某一页具体图表。😵‍💫
当你：
- 胸有成竹底气十足 👉 简洁精准作答。
- 确实不知道答案 👉 大方坦承目前未知，并表示会后跟进探讨。

![Respond concisely](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FY9waZYX0AQ83qj.jpg)

---

*Manage your pace*
Don't let tough questions disrupt your pace, e.g.,
Back-to-the-future questions:
👉 "I will cover that in a next few slides."
Insist on unnecessary details:
👉 "More than happy to discuss offline."
Schmidhuber-based questions:
👉 "Our work differs ..."

*掌控节奏，兵来将挡 (Manage your pace)*
不要让尖锐或偏离主线的问题打乱你的节奏，例如：
“超前剧透型”问题：
👉 “我正好在接下来的几张幻灯片中会讲到这一点。”
纠缠于细枝末节的技术抬杠：
👉 “非常乐意在会后与您线下深入探讨。”
施密德胡伯式“这工作别人早就做过”式提问（Schmidhuber-style questions）：
👉 “我们工作的核心不同之处在于……”

![Manage your pace](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FY9wa3nX0AAWKJf.jpg)

---

*Check if your audience is following along*
❌ "Any questions for this part?", "Is this clear?", "Does it make sense?"
No questions asked 👉 clear talk?
Nah 🫤, it may imply that your audience just gave up or simply don't care.
Instead,
✅ "Can I have two questions here?"

*确认听众是否真正跟上了进度 (Check if audience is following)*
❌ “这部分大家有什么问题吗？”、“讲清楚了吗？”、“大家听懂了吗？”
没人提问 👉 说明大家全懂了？
绝非如此 🫤，这往往意味着听众已经彻底放弃跟上节奏，或者根本不在意。
相反，应当尝试：
✅ “这里我想先征集两个针对这部分的提问，有哪位愿意先提吗？”

![Check audience](https://pbs.twimg.com/tweet_video_thumb/FY9wdpTWYAEddLa.jpg)

---

*Anticipate and provide questions*
Anticipate what questions will be asked and practice, practice, practice.
Pro tip for conference presentation:
👉 Provide one or two questions for your session chairs.
It helps them ask good questions and helps you respond well. 😎

*预判问题，甚至主动为 Session Chair 提供问题 (Anticipate and provide questions)*
预判可能会被问到的潜在问题，反复练习作答。
学术会议演讲的高阶秘籍：
👉 提前为主持人（Session Chair）准备一到两个优质问题。
这能帮主持人免于冷场的尴尬，也能让你在台上应对自如。😎
